## Stock photos

under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free to use for commercial
and non-commercial purposes, no permission needed, attribution appreciated but not
required. None of them is an Unsplash+ (paid) image. They are credited in the site footer.

Each photo was downloaded at 2400 px wide on 28 September 2026, cropped to the 5:4 step
frame and compressed (`npm run assets`); nothing else was changed. The downloaded files
are kept locally in `AkcanutAssets/stock/` (not in git).
